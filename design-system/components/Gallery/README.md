# Gallery

A mosaic of real park photographs, with captions on a navy scrim strip.

- **Provide:** `images` (each with `src`, `alt`, `caption` and `layout`: `normal`, `wide` or `tall`) and `rowHeight` (220 by default).
- Mix one or two `tall` and `wide` photos into every six, and fill every row.
- Captions say what and where, not adjectives: "Reception on the lawn".
