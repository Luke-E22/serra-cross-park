# Ornament

The dotted O from the display face, used as a small mark and as a divider rule.

- **Provide:** `variant` (`mark` or `rule`), `tone` (`accent` ochre by default, `ink`, or `paper` on dark grounds), and `size` (the glyph height in px).
- Use the rule under centered section titles, and the mark in hero eyebrows, timeline markers and the footer's legal line.
- One ornament per block. Never as a list bullet or a background pattern.
