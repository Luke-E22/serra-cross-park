# Hero

The title slide: a full-bleed park photograph with the page title in `display-xl` over a navy scrim.

- **Provide:** `image` and `imageAlt`, a `title` of two to five words, an optional `eyebrow`, a `lede` of one or two sentences, `actions` (one `primary` button with `onDark`, and at most one `secondary`), a `caption` for the photo, `focal` (CSS object-position) to keep the Cross in frame, `header` (a `SiteHeader` with `overlay`), and `height` (`full` on Home, `tall` on section pages, `short` on utility pages).
- Body text always sits on `scrim`, so it reads on any photo. Don't remove the scrim or move text outside the content block.
- Under 720px the photo stacks above the text on navy.
- Use real park photographs, with the Cross or the ocean in the upper half of the frame.
