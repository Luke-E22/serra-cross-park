# FeatureCard

A photo card with a sign-band label, a title, one line of text and a link. The whole card is clickable.

- **Provide:** `image`, `imageAlt`, `eyebrow` (one word: Weddings, History, Give), `tone` (`navy` for weddings, `green` for history, `ochre` for giving, `brick` for visiting), `title`, `text` (one sentence), `href` and `linkLabel`.
- Use three across on Home to route people to Weddings, History and Give.
- Photos crop to 4:3, so keep the subject centered.
