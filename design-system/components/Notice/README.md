# Notice

A short practical note people must not miss: no water on site, gates lock at eight.

- **Provide:** `title` (the fact), `children` (what to do about it), `tone` (`info`, or `caution` for hard limits) and an optional `icon`.
- Caution titles and icons use `danger` (brick) and always come with words.
- Use one or two per page, next to the content they qualify.
