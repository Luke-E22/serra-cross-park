Serra Cross Park Special Event Terms and Conditions

Thank you for choosing Serra Cross Park for your special event. Please read the following

terms and conditions carefully. By booking an event at Serra Cross Park, you agree to adhere to

these terms.

1. General Terms of Use

1.1 Reservation and Payment

To reserve Serra Cross Park for a special event, clients must submit a completed reservation

form along with the full payment for the reservation. Your reservation is not confirmed until

payment has been received and processed.

1.2 Non-Refundable Payment

All payments are non-refundable. Clients may reschedule the event date once, subject to

availability and the payment of any applicable fees. Any cancellation of the event will result in

the forfeiture of the payment.

1.3 Rescheduling

Clients may reschedule the event once without incurring any fees, subject to availability. A

request to reschedule the date and time of your event must be submitted via email to

admin@serracrosspark.com.

2. Client Responsibilities

2.1 Event Setup and Cleanup

The client is responsible for the setup and breakdown of all event items, including tables, chairs,

decor, and other rentals. The client is also responsible for cleaning up after the event and

removing all items, including trash, décor, and equipment, by the end of the reservation period.

2.2 Insurance Requirement

For events involving catered food, beverages, and/or a reception, the client must provide proof

of a 1-day, $1 million liability insurance policy covering the event. Proof of insurance must be

submitted to admin@serracrosspark.com at least seven days prior to the event.

2.3 Time Management

The client must adhere to the time allotted for the event, including setup, event duration, and

cleanup. Any overrun of the reserved time will incur an additional charge of $300 per hour.

2.4 Rental Equipment and Décor

The client is responsible for providing their own rentals (e.g., chairs, tables, tents) and décor. All

rental equipment must be removed at the end of the event, and the client agrees to hold Serra

Cross Park harmless for any damage or loss to such items.

2.5 Alcohol Service

No self-served alcohol is allowed. Alcohol must be served by a licensed provider (bartender,

caterer). The client is responsible for ensuring that all applicable alcohol laws and regulations

are followed, including age restrictions.

2.6 No Synthetic Confetti or Petals

Synthetic confetti or petals is strictly prohibited. Clients may use biodegradable or natural

confetti or petals.. Failure to comply with this policy will result in additional cleanup fees.

3. Property Conditions

3.1 Public Access

The park gates remain open to the public from dawn to dusk. Due to safety, the gates to the

Serra Cross Park are not allowed to be locked from dawn to dusk. The client acknowledges that

there may be other individuals in the park during the reserved event time. The client is permitted

to put up signs, hire security, station a volunteer at the gate, and notify the public of an event

taking place.

3.2 Water Source

There is no water source on the property. Clients must make arrangements for water as

necessary for the event.

3.3 Restroom Facilities

Serra Cross Park does not provide restroom facilities. Clients are required to arrange for

portable restrooms if needed for their event. If your event is on a weekend, restrooms can be

delivered Friday and picked up Monday.

3.4 Lighting

There is no lighting on the property, including in the parking lot. Clients are responsible for

providing their own lighting if their event extends into the evening.

3.6 Trash and Waste

The client is responsible for disposing of all trash and waste generated during the event. The

client must ensure that the park is left in the same condition it was found. Failure to remove

trash may result in a one time trash removal fee.

3.7 Parking

Parking is available for 35 vehicles in designated areas within the gates to the park. The venue

is not responsible for any loss, theft, or damage to vehicles or personal items while on the

premises.

4. Prohibited Activities

4.1 Illegal Activities

Any illegal activities, including drug use, underage drinking, or unlawful behavior, are strictly

prohibited and will result in immediate termination of the event.

4.2 Firearms and Weapons

The possession or use of firearms, explosives, or other dangerous weapons is strictly prohibited

in the park, unless authorized by law.

4.3 Smoking, Fireworks or Open Flames

Smoking, fireworks, open flames, or any form of fire are strictly prohibited on property.

4.4 Vehicles on Lawn

For the preservation of the lawn and surrounding areas, no vehicles are allowed on the grass.

Please use designated parking and loading areas. Thank you for your cooperation.

5. Liability and Indemnity

5.1 Client Liability

The client assumes full responsibility for any accidents, injuries, or damages that occur during

the event. The client agrees to indemnify and hold harmless Serra Cross Park, its employees,

volunteers, and affiliates from any claims arising from the event.

5.2 Park Liability

Serra Cross Park is not responsible for any personal injury, damage, theft, or loss of personal

property that occurs during the event. The client assumes all risks associated with holding an

event on park property.

By making a reservation and holding an event at Serra Cross Park, you agree to abide by the

above Terms and Conditions.
